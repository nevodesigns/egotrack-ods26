# EgoTrack

Turns a trader's paper notebook into records she can check, keep safe and share.

Most traders in Nigeria sell on credit and write it in a notebook. The book works until a customer disputes a debt, or the shop loses the book to fire, flood or theft. EgoTrack keeps the notebook. The trader takes one photo of a page. An AI model copies what is written. Plain code redoes every sum and flags anything that does not add up. The trader confirms. From that she gets who owes her, a WhatsApp reminder in her own language, and a trust record anyone can check by code.

Built live for the Fintech track at the Ogun Innovate Hackathon, Ogun Tech Hub, Kobape, Abeokuta, 25 November 2026. Shown first for traders in Ogun State, starting with Kuto and Itoku markets in Abeokuta.

## The one flow

Snap a page, check the rows, confirm, see who owes you, remind them, prove it with a trust record.
